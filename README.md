### 🛒 [E-Commerce Customer & Product Analytics](https://github.com/divanshujain17/E-Commerce_Customer_and_Product_Analytics)
> **SQL · Python · Power BI · RFM · CLV · Cohort Analysis**

End-to-end analytics on the Brazilian Olist dataset (~100K orders, $13.6M revenue). 14 analytical phases covering customer segmentation, RFM scoring, cohort retention, product performance, CLV, geographic intelligence, and delivery operations. Built Power BI report with 6 dashboard pages for executive, customer, product, time-series, CLV, and geographic views.
